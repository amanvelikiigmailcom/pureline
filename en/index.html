FAQ
How much does cleaning cost in Astana?

On the Astana market, a general clean costs around 600 ₸ per m² on average. But that price usually doesn't include washing windows, cleaning carpets and sofas with chemicals and professional equipment, washing and ironing curtains, cleaning the inside of appliances, washing dishes and collecting dirty laundry. We offer an honest price of 1,000 ₸ per m², which already includes all of these extras that other companies charge separately for - and more. For the same scope of work, the average price at other companies in Astana starts from 1,500 ₸ per m².

What does a deep apartment cleaning in Astana include?

A Pureline deep cleaning of an apartment in Astana includes: thorough floor care, dusting and wet-cleaning of all surfaces, washing windows inside and out (where accessible), cleaning bathrooms and appliances, cleaning carpets and sofas with special equipment, washing curtains, plus washing dishes (up to 5 kg) and ironing (up to 20 items).

Do you offer in-home sofa cleaning in Astana?

Yes, we clean sofas, mattresses and carpets at your home in Astana using extraction equipment. Our safe, hypoallergenic products remove old stains and odors and are suitable for children's rooms and homes with pets. Furniture dries in 4-6 hours and is ready to use the same day.

What other services do you provide?

We also help with moving: we'll pack up your belongings, prepare the apartment after move-out, clean and get the new apartment ready, and put everything away in its place - making the whole move completely seamless for you. You decide where you want to live, and we take care of all the hassle that comes with it.

How is premium cleaning different from regular cleaning?

Premium cleaning means an 80-point checklist, certified chlorine-free eco products, HEPA equipment and our own manager on site for quality control. Unlike regular cleaning, we back it with a guarantee and a contract that covers the safety of your property. You pay for a predictable, five-star-hotel result, not improvisation.

Which residential complexes in Astana do you serve?

We work all across Astana, including the Left Bank and premium complexes such as Highvill, BI Village, Karaotkel and others. We also clean cottages, townhouses and private houses in the Yesil district and the suburbs. We come with our own equipment and products, even on the day you contact us.

How do I book a same-day cleaning?

To book a same-day cleaning in Astana, call us or message us on WhatsApp - we're available daily from 09:00 to 21:00.
